Explainable Multimodal ECG Intelligence System for Early Cardiac Risk Assessment

🔴 Problem Statement

* ECG interpretation is often dependent on trained medical professionals and can be time-consuming.
* Subtle cardiac abnormalities may be difficult to identify, especially when analyzing large volumes of ECG recordings.
* Most existing AI systems focus only on classifying ECG signals, rather than providing an overall cardiac risk assessment.
* AI predictions can be difficult for clinicians to trust when the reasoning behind the prediction is unclear.
* There is a need for an explainable multimodal AI system that combines ECG signals with relevant patient information to identify potential cardiac risk and explain the factors behind its prediction.

🟢 Proposed Solution

* Collect ECG signals along with available patient information such as age, sex, heart rate, and relevant clinical parameters.
* Preprocess ECG signals by removing noise, normalizing them, and segmenting the recordings.
* Use a 1D-CNN/LSTM or Transformer to extract important patterns from ECG signals.
* Use a separate ML model such as XGBoost/Neural Network to process structured patient information.
* Combine ECG features and patient features using a multimodal fusion layer.
* Predict cardiac abnormality/risk level based on the available dataset and target labels.
* Use Explainable AI (SHAP/attention/feature importance) to show which ECG patterns and patient factors influenced the prediction.
* Display the ECG waveform, predicted risk, confidence, and important contributing factors through a web-based dashboard.
* Evaluate the system using precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, sensitivity, and specificity.
