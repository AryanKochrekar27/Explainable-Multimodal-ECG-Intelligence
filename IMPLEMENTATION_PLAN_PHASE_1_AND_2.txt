1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & WORKSPACE GROUND-TRUTH DISCOVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Based on direct inspection of the workspace, preprocessed manifests, and data audit
records (in `data/processed/` and `reports/FINAL_DATA_PREPROCESSING_REPORT.md`):

1. ECG Data Representation:
   - Modality: 12-lead paper ECG scans stored as high-resolution images (2213x1572 px).
   - Processed size: 224x224x3 RGB tensors normalized to [0, 1].
   - Volume: 928 image files spanning 491 unique patient scans across 4 classes:
     * Normal (Class 0): 142 unique scans (284 files total)
     * Abnormal Heartbeat (Class 1): 233 unique scans (233 files total)
     * Myocardial Infarction (Class 2): 30 unique scans (239 files total)
     * Post-MI History (Class 3): 86 unique scans (172 files total)
   - Scientific Rule: As these are digitized paper image scans, they will be modeled
     strictly using 2D Computer Vision architectures (2D-CNN / ResNet). We will NOT
     apply 1D signal filters, 1D-CNNs, or claim time-series modeling on image pixels.

2. Clinical Tabular Representation:
   - Volume: 4,327 clinical records across 19 engineered features.
   - Files: `X_train_clinical.csv` (N=3,028), `X_val_clinical.csv` (N=649),
     `X_test_clinical.csv` (N=650), with fitted `models/clinical_preprocessor.joblib`.
   - Feature Characteristics: Features show synthetic uniform distribution; cardiac
     troponin and blood pressure metrics are decoupled from true physiology.

3. CSV-to-Image Linkage & Group-Aware Separation:
   - The CSV column `ECG_Image_Path` contains fictional pointers (`ecg_1.jpg`–`ecg_4327.jpg`)
     whereas physical images are named `HB(1).jpg`, `MI(1).jpg`, etc.
   - Non-Negotiable Rule: We refuse to invent fake/random image-to-clinical pairings.
   - Dual-track solution:
     * Track A: Train and validate ECG-Only Vision Model on the verified group-aware
       image splits (`image_split_manifest.csv`) with zero duplicate-image leakage.
     * Track B: Train and validate Clinical-Only Tabular Model on the clinical partitions.
     * Track C: Controlled Multimodal Fusion Architecture evaluation using aligned cohort
       protocols and ablation testing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. NON-NEGOTIABLE SCIENTIFIC & ENGINEERING CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. No Data Fabrication: Never invent missing CSV-to-image links or patient IDs.
2. Zero Split Leakage: Maintain strict group-aware separation by image MD5 hash
   so that duplicated scans never span train, validation, or test partitions.
3. Test Set Isolation: The test set will NOT be used for early stopping, model
   selection, hyperparameter tuning, threshold calibration, or SHAP calculations.
4. Model Modality Fidelity: An image CNN will be described honestly as 2D computer
   vision on paper ECG printouts, never misrepresented as numerical 1D time-series processing.
5. Realistic Clinical Scope: Results will be reported as research prototypes for
   diagnostic classification, without unsupported claims of clinical readiness or
   causal event prediction.
